Word Mix sounds

Tap any sound to hear it. Where there are two versions, the one you played last is marked as your pick, and your picks are listed at the bottom to send back to me.

Turn your volume up. On iPhone and iPad, switch off silent mode or nothing will play. Every sound is made live by the phone, so the game stays small and works offline.

Board startup

Plays on the big screen, or on the host phone, as a round begins.

Dice shake

Letter cubes tumbling around the dome: plastic ticks and wooden knocks.

Drop and settle

A crisp clack for each of the 16 tiles as the grid locks into place.

3-beat countdown

Beep, beep, BEEP. Everyone starts on the last one.

While you play

These play on each player's own phone.

Letter chime

Each letter you swipe through chimes a little higher, so you can hear how long your word is.

Word found

A normal word, 3 to 5 letters.

Long word found

6 letters or more.

Wrong move

Swiping to a letter that isn't touching, or one you've already used.

Not a word

A short error shake.

Already found

A soft boing.

Try it
Swipe across touching letters. Release to submit.

In this practice grid every path of 3 or more letters counts as a word, so you can hear the word sounds. Swipe the same path twice to hear Already found. The real game checks the dictionary.

Music and the last 10 seconds

Background music plays on the big screen, or on the host phone, during a round.

Background groove

A light, mid-tempo jazz-pop loop: soft drums, bass and electric piano.

Ticking overlay

Plays over the music for the last 10 seconds.

Time's up

Ends the round.

0:10

The last 10 seconds speeds the music up, adds your chosen ticking overlay, plays urgent beeps for the final 3 seconds, then your chosen Time's up sound.

Results reveal

Plays during the word-by-word reveal.

Shared word swish

A sharp swish as a word more than one player found appears. Word Mix doesn't cross shared words out, so the swish plays as they appear.

Unique word sparkle

A golden chime flourish for a word only one player found.

Score counter

A fast scoreboard tick as points roll up.
