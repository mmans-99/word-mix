# Word Mix

A word-grid game for phones. Swipe across touching letters to find words.

- Play solo, pass and play (2 to 6 players), or phone to phone with no internet
- Classic, Blitz, Zen and Daily modes on 4x4, 5x5 or 6x6 boards
- Works offline once installed (add it to your home screen)

Play: https://mmans-99.github.io/word-mix/
