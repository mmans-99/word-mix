# Word Mix

A word-grid game for phones. Swipe across touching letters to find words.

- Play solo, pass and play (2 to 8 players), or Party phone to phone with no internet (up to 8 players, with an optional big-screen host)
- Classic, Blitz, Survival, Rarity, Zen and Daily modes on 4x4, 5x5 or 6x6 boards, with Normal and Easy boards
- Child profile: double points for a child's own words, plus an always-there Hint button in Party and pass-and-play
- Party results as a live bar battle: words light up on the board, bars coloured by place, a 👑 Winner and 💩 Loser riding on the bars, personal bests, round highlights and session trivia
- Two colour schemes, Warm and Party, each in light or dark
- 90-second rounds by default in Classic and Party
- Leaderboards on this phone and shared with everyone you've played with
- Works offline once installed (add it to your home screen)
